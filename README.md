# KGL Cash Conversion Analysis

Cash-first financial analysis of KGL focused on liquidity, working capital, CCC, CFS, profitability pressure, and debt servicing capacity.

## Live version

[Open GitHub Pages site](https://mbimarban.github.io/kgl-cash-conversion-analysis/)

## Project overview

This project presents a CFO-style review of KGL for FY2022, FY2023, FY2024, and 9M 2025.

The goal is not only to assess accounting profit, but above all to understand how effectively sales convert into cash.

## Key takeaway

KGL improved strongly in 2023, but in 2024 and 9M 2025 sales converted much weaker into cash due to lower margins, higher working capital pressure, and weaker debt service coverage.

## Main analytical focus

- liquidity and short-term balance sheet safety
- receivables, inventory, and payables dynamics
- cash conversion cycle (CCC)
- cash flow sales (CFS)
- cash flow from operations (CFO) vs net profit
- debt service pressure and capital structure
- management commentary vs underlying numbers

## Main conclusion

The core issue is not only lower profitability.  
The bigger problem is that operating performance is converting less efficiently into cash, mainly because of margin compression and working capital absorption.
